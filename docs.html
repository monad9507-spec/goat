01

GPU MINING

Connect your wallet and run the WebGPU miner. Every SHA-256 attempt is made locally on your graphics card. A valid proof is bound to your wallet and the latest challenge, so another wallet cannot copy it.

02

DIFFICULTY

Difficulty begins at 30 BITS. It retargets after every eight successful mints and can move by only one bit per retarget. During inactivity it falls by one bit every ten seconds until it reaches the current supply floor.

START30 BITS
ABSOLUTE MAX40 BITS
IDLE DECAY−1 / 10 SEC
RETARGET8 MINTS

The floor rises by one bit every 500 mints and stops permanently at 35 BITS. The contract cannot repeat the uncontrolled 50-BIT spike.

03

04

IPFS METADATA

Each token points to an immutable-style IPFS path ending in its token number and .json. The owner may correct the base URI before it is permanently frozen. Royalty is fixed at 5% in the collection contract.

05

STAY SAFE

The website never asks for your seed phrase or private key. Mining only computes hashes until a proof is found; minting happens only after you confirm a contract transaction in your wallet. TURBO mode uses more power and may increase GPU temperature.
