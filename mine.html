THE PROTOCOL

YOU DON'T BUY A GOAT.
YOU DISCOVER ONE.

Your GPU searches for a SHA-256 proof tied to your wallet and the latest on-chain challenge. When the hash beats the current target, the contract verifies it and mints your Hash Goat.

The first 500 goats are free. Afterwards, the price rises by 0.0001 ETH every 500 mints. Artwork and metadata are stored on IPFS.
